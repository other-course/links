💡 How to Find Students Who Want to Learn Coding

Decide who your ideal students are:

  • 👦 School students (8th–12th grade)
  • 🎓 College students (engineering, BCA, etc.)
  • 💼 Working professionals who want to switch to IT
  • 🧑‍💻 Beginners with no coding background

Each group hangs out in different places online — so your approach changes based on this.

🔹 Social Media Platforms
  • Instagram & Facebook: Post mini-lessons, use hashtags like #LearnCoding, and join groups like “Learn Programming”. Offer free demos.
  • LinkedIn: Connect with students, college clubs, and share student success stories.
  • YouTube: Upload short tutorials or live demo sessions with a sign-up link.
  • Telegram / WhatsApp: Create a group like “Free Coding Classes – Beginners Batch” and invite learners.

Approach schools or local institutes to conduct free coding awareness workshops. After the session, interested students usually sign up for your regular classes.

Register on these tutor websites to reach active learners:

  • UrbanPro
  • Superprof
  • TeacherOn
  • LearnPick
  • Classgap

Use Facebook or Instagram ads targeting:

  • Age group: 14–25
  • Interests: “Coding”, “Python”, “Web Development”
  • Location: India or your city

Even ₹200–₹500/day can bring high-quality leads if your ad is engaging.

Encourage your existing students to refer friends. Offer referral discounts or free extra sessions as rewards.

Free sessions build trust. Try:

  • “3 Days Free Bootcamp: Learn to Build Your First Website”
  • “1-Hour Free Class: How Coding Can Get You a Job”

Use Google Forms or a simple HTML landing page like: “Join My Free Coding Class” with inputs for Name, Email, and WhatsApp number. Then follow up manually or using WhatsApp automation.

Answer coding questions on Quora and Reddit. Share your knowledge, and invite learners to join your classes.
